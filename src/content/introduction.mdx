## Build the reader journey

Begin with the task that brings most readers to your documentation, then make
the next useful step obvious.

<CardGroup cols="2">
  <Card title="Deliver a first result" icon="party-horn" href="/quickstart">
    Turn prerequisites, setup, one realistic task, and verification into a short path.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organize by reader goal" icon="grid-round" href="/customization">
    Group related tasks and references around how people use your product.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Document the API" icon="code-simple" href="/api">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Use focused components" icon="wrench" href="/components">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Publish from one source

Thally turns the same reviewed content into HTML, search, agent discovery files,
and a read-only MCP surface. Add only the locales your readers need, and enable
optional Markdown page URLs when they fit your access model.

<Note type="info" title="Start with the product, not the template">
  Replace this page with the questions, terminology, and workflows your readers
  already use. Keep `docs.json` focused on the path between those tasks.
</Note>
